About Us

Where East Meets West

The World Cultural Exchange Association was founded in 2025 in Regensburg, Germany by seven passionate individuals united by a single vision: to build genuine, lasting bridges between Chinese and Western cultures.

Through workshops, exhibitions, language programs, and community events, we create spaces where traditions are shared, curiosity is celebrated, and new friendships flourish.

Our Mission

Three Pillars of Connection

Cultural Exchange

We create immersive experiences — from calligraphy workshops and traditional tea ceremonies to European art tours — that bring people into direct, joyful contact with one another's living heritage.

01

Education

Through language courses, lecture series, and cross-cultural mentorship programs, we empower individuals to understand and appreciate the depth of both Chinese and Western intellectual traditions.

02

Community

We build a welcoming, inclusive network in Regensburg and beyond — a community where Chinese and European members support one another, celebrate together, and grow together as global citizens.
